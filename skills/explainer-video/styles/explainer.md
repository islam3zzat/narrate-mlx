# Style: explainer (the default)

For a learner who wants to understand a topic. A script without `style` is this style. Read `references/presentation.md` first; this file only restates the shape.

## Shape
1. `hook`: a question or surprise the viewer already cares about, then what the video covers.
2. `map`: the parts of the topic on one picture (flow, graph, steps) before any detail.
3. `example`: one concrete case before the abstraction.
4. `concept`: name and generalise it. At most 3 to 4 new terms per scene; define each at first use.
5. `close`: a recap of three items and a retrieval prompt ("Before the next part, try to say why ..."); name the next part if there is one.

## Numbers
- Length: depth sets it (15 to 60 words a scene, 2 to 40 scenes). Split into parts (`<id>-part1`) rather than cramming.
- Speed 1.0. One analogy per hard idea, and say where it stops working.
- Any visual type. Prefer a diagram when the content has structure.

## Do not
- Do not justify the style by "learning styles"; there is no evidence for them.
- Do not use `style` fields you do not need: no ledger, no sections required.
